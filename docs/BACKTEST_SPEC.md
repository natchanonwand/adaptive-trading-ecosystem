# Deterministic backtest specification

## Reproducibility contract

A run pins strategy/risk/arithmetic versions, instrument and calendar versions, dataset manifest, cost schedule, simulator version, environment lock, initial USD cash (default 100,000), evaluation windows, and event ordering. No network or wall-clock access is allowed in the domain simulation. V0 uses no randomness. Any future stochastic model must record its algorithm and seed. Equal manifests produce byte-identical normalized decision/fill/ledger streams; operational IDs and run creation times are excluded from the normalized checksum.

## Event time and fills

Use the shared long-only strategy, risk, and actual-fill bracket calculation. Process each timestamp in this order: effective metadata/session and financing events; chronological broker/price events and active SL/TP for existing exposure; pending exceptional exits; eligible earlier entry intents and post-fill bracket installation; account valuation and loss controls; completed-bar availability events; lexically ordered new entries. A newly created intent cannot execute at that timestamp. Within equal quote timestamps use the source sequence; if it is absent and changes SL-vs-TP outcome, mark ordering unresolved and fail quote qualification rather than inventing chronology. A sell execution can only reduce remaining long exposure.

Two explicitly labeled fidelity levels:

- **Quote replay (qualification):** use timestamped bid/ask, known availability, and validated execution/cost model. Entry at first eligible ask strictly after decision time plus one adverse tick. Compute initial_price_risk from this actual simulated fill/VWAP and fixed stop, then raw TP = entry + 2 × initial_price_risk, rounded upward to tick. A long SL triggers at bid ≤ SL and fills at min(SL, executable bid) minus one adverse tick. TP triggers at bid ≥ TP and fills at executable bid minus one adverse tick under the baseline broker-market-trigger assumption; broker-specific behavior must be validated under D01. Costs/slippage may make realized net R differ from +2R. Actual chronological quotes determine which active level triggers first; the first trigger consumes the applicable remaining quantity and cancels/reconciles the sibling. SL/TP cannot trigger before installation. Baseline simulation installs the derived TP synchronously after the fill event, before the next quote; fault scenarios model the five-second receipt-to-confirmation deadline, rejection, partial fills, and race handling. Exceptional exits fill at eligible bid minus one adverse tick. Orders cannot fill during closed sessions. Spread is embodied in bid/ask prices.
- **OHLC exploratory (non-qualifying):** completed bid bars plus pinned spread. Entry at next eligible bar open strictly after decision time; if timestamps coincide, use a documented positive latency/open-price approximation. Ask = bid open + spread; entry and market/stop/TP exits include one adverse tick. Compute the target from actual simulated entry after slippage. Process known opening gaps first for already active brackets: open ≤ SL exits at open minus one tick; open ≥ TP exits at open minus one tick. A known opening exit is not ambiguous. For remaining exposure, test low ≤ SL and high ≥ TP. If both touch and their sequence is unknown, assume adverse SL first and set `intrabar_ambiguous=true` on event, fill, and trade; stop fills at SL minus one tick. If only SL touches, fill at SL minus one tick; if only TP touches, fill at TP minus one tick. The same conservative rule applies to bar-open entries after target creation. A boundary/safety exit with unknowable intrabar ordering also records ambiguity and uses the adverse feasible outcome. No inferred OHLC path may qualify as chronological quote evidence.

SL/TP targets do not trail. Partial-fill target versions and exceptional completion use [STRATEGY_V0.md](STRATEGY_V0.md). Normal exit reasons are TAKE_PROFIT/STOP_LOSS; RISK_SAFETY, OPERATOR_FLATTEN, PROTECTION_FAILURE, and EVALUATION_BOUNDARY remain separately labeled. Diagnostic ambiguity is independent of exit reason: an ambiguous stop remains STOP_LOSS with its ambiguity flag. All OHLC exploratory evidence remains non-qualifying even when a particular trade is unambiguous.

Reject entries beyond five-minute expiry, with bad freshness, or violating risk limits. No same-close fill. SL rounds down and TP rounds up under the arithmetic contract; fills use adverse directional tick rounding. Model commissions per fill, including minimum fees, and financing at versioned broker rollover instants, including multi-day charges. Costs cannot silently default to zero. Model partial fills, rejections, latency, disconnects, and unknown submissions in fault scenarios; the baseline quote model assumes full liquidity at requested volume and explicitly discloses that assumption.

## Data and leakage prevention

Fail validation for duplicate/conflicting bars, invalid OHLC, unknown timezone, crossed quotes, or unversioned contracts. Label scheduled closures; do not fill unexpected gaps. Reset entry warm-up after missing expected bars. Data corrections create new manifests. Macro research uses original release vintages and first available times; revised hindsight data must not drive historical decisions. Model fitting and parameter selection see only training windows.

Chronological defaults: first 60% of calendar duration for development, next 20% validation, final 20% locked out-of-sample; all assets use identical UTC boundaries. Register exact windows before evaluation. Allow a preceding warm-up period without orders. Start each evaluation window flat with its stated initial capital; flatten at the last executable quote at or before the end for closed-trade metrics, label this EVALUATION_BOUNDARY exit, and include costs. If any position cannot be closed or valued at the boundary, mark evidence incomplete and fail qualification. Walk-forward research is supplementary; do not repeatedly tune on locked data. A candidate changed after observing holdout results requires a new untouched holdout or prospective evidence plan approved before testing.

## Outputs and qualification

Produce manifests, validation findings, event checksums, intents/rejections, orders/fills, ledger, equity curve, per-asset and combined metrics, regime diagnostics if available, and machine-readable plus human-readable reports. Include zero-trade assets and missing-data intervals, actual-entry/SL/TP lineage, price RR versus net R, reason-specific exits, and intrabar-ambiguous counts.

Cost stress doubles spread width around the observed bid/ask midpoint, doubles commission and financing debits (positive credits unchanged), and increases adverse slippage from one to two ticks per execution. Re-run strategy and risk decisions with the stressed stream; never merely subtract estimated costs from baseline returns. See [TEST_PLAN.md](TEST_PLAN.md) for evidence thresholds. Sensitivity analyses are reported as research and cannot silently replace the frozen candidate.

The historical provider, coverage, real spreads, rollover costs, tick valuation, margin, and session details are unresolved D01/D02. Exploratory runs are possible only after implementation authorization; credible qualification remains blocked until these inputs are validated.
